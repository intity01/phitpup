What PhitPup does

Forgot to switch the keyboard to Thai and typed l;ylfu? PhitPup sees you meant สวัสดี and fixes it.

  • A small slip under the cursor suggests the fix. Press Tab to take it, Esc to hide it.
  • Shift+Backspace fixes the last word, or the text you selected. Press it again right away to
    get your word back.
  • Shift+Esc tells PhitPup never to suggest that word again.

It lives next to the clock as an orange p. Click it for the settings: pick suggest, auto or
hotkey-only, the Thai layout (Kedmanee or Pattachote), programs to leave alone and words to
skip.

It stays out of password fields, password managers, Remote Desktop and full-screen games.

Why might antivirus warn about it?
To fix a word, PhitPup has to see the keys you press and type for you, which is what keyloggers
do too, so some antivirus programs warn about anything like it. PhitPup keeps only the word you
are typing, in memory, and never sends it anywhere. All of its code is public.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Installs for your account only, with no admin rights, and can be
removed from Settings > Apps.
